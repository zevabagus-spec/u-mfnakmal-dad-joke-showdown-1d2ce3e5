A program that picks the strongest original dad joke from a small curated pool — scored against the published judge brief (dad-voice, clean pun, freshness, tightness, rhythm, in that order) — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`. One joke, at most 200 characters, built to land the biggest groan. The winning joke: "I told my wife she should embrace her mistakes. She gave me a hug." The pun ("embrace" — accept / hug) lands on the first read with no explaining needed, the payoff is the very last word, and the scene is a father talking at home. No text addressed to the judge: the brief's first criterion is that the entry reads as a dad joke a father would tell, and a plea to the judge is not that.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
